§ guardrails · the walls

Yunaki can read, check, and draft. It can never send, sign, or file.

Your files hold your clients' lives. So the rules below are not policies we ask an AI to follow — they are built into the software itself, and tested every time it changes.

§01 · what can never happen

Structural, not prompted.

01

Nothing sends without you

you press send

Every email Yunaki writes waits as a draft. You read it, you press send. The part of the system that writes drafts cannot send them — that's how it's built, not a setting.

02

Nothing is signed or filed

your attorney's act

Yunaki cannot touch a signature field — the software refuses to fill one, period. There is no e-filing and no auto-submit anywhere in the product. Filing is your attorney's act, always.

03

No made-up answers

blank over guess

If a date is unreadable, it stays blank. If an answer can't be judged, Yunaki says "can't tell" instead of guessing. When something goes wrong, it fails toward caution — never toward a pass.

04

Your firm's data is yours alone

walled off, twice

Every lookup is limited to your firm — once in the software and again in the database itself. Another firm asking for your record gets the same answer as asking for one that doesn't exist.

05

Clients don't see each other's answers

one case, two halves

A petitioner and a beneficiary can share a case without sharing answers. Each gets their own link, their own questions, their own half of the file.

06

No client details in our logs

numbers, not names

Our system logs never contain a name, an answer, an address, or a document. When we debug a problem, we see case numbers and counts — not your client.

07

It can only do what's on its list

a short, fixed list

Each assistant has a fixed, short list of things it is allowed to do. Anything else is refused automatically. Every run has a hard limit, and a run that hits it says so instead of pretending it finished.

08

Everything on the record

a full paper trail

Every run is recorded: what it read, what it did, in what order, and how it ended. You can see the same trail in the app, case by case.

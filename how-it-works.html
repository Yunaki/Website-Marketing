§ how it works · architecture

The method is fixed. The memory evolves.

Most legal AI puts a language model in charge of everything, including what it's worst at. Yunaki is built the other way around.

§01 · the core rule

Facts never come from a language model.

Poverty guidelines are a table. A means test is arithmetic. A form edition is a version string. A rules engine gets these wrong 0% of the time, by construction.

WHY IT MATTERS · TRUST

76% of the immigration bar avoids AI over hallucination fears. Here, that failure is structurally impossible.

WHY IT MATTERS · CHANGE

Rules change? We update a versioned playbook the same day. No retraining, no guessing.

§02 · the system

Six subsystems, one loop.

01

Client portal intake

party-scoped, by construction

Clients answer through magic links scoped to their side of the case — the petitioner never sees the beneficiary's half. Drafts stay private until Submit, and every answer lands as a typed, provenance-stamped record, not a PDF in an inbox.

02

Deterministic extraction

null over guess

Passports, green cards, EADs, I-94s, and civil documents are read field by field. Absent, blank, or illegible is recorded as null — never completed with a plausible value. A wrong document in the slot is refused, not misread.

03

The case check

measured, biased against accusing

Every answer is judged complete, incomplete, or undeterminable — the third verdict exists because a false accusation is worse than a missed one. A deterministic crosscheck compares the file to the documents and flags field names, never values. The judge is replayed against a labeled golden set on every change, including adversarial injection attempts.

04

USCIS knowledge as data

verified, cited, versioned

30 visa classifications: required forms, editions, supporting documents, and filing channels — each verified against official sources with a citation and a date. Anything unverified says so. The edition is data, not model weights.

05

Firm memory

work product, encoded

Every intake answer, document, and outcome becomes a structured, queryable case record. Ask @yunaki in Slack and it answers from your firm's own file — with citations to it. It stays yours.

06

Hard guardrails

structural, not prompted

An agent here can read, judge, and draft — it can never send, sign, or file. Every outbound message waits as a draft for attorney approval. Signature fields are structurally unfillable. UPL boundaries are hard-coded, not prompted. The full list →

§01 · the problem

Legal paperwork fails at industrial scale.

  • The same client data is retyped into 5 to 15 forms. No tool checks that it matches.
  • When an attorney leaves, the knowledge of what worked leaves too.
  • Most errors are deterministic: wrong edition, bad math, mismatched dates.

§02 · how it works

The client uploads. The engine reads, checks, and asks.

Watch it run: a passport goes in, a checked case file comes out.

ON EVERY ANSWER

The case check judges each client answer complete, incomplete, or undeterminable — it is built to say "I can't tell" before it will ever accuse. Conflicts between the file and the documents are flagged by field name, never by quoting your client back.

ON EVERY SEND

Nothing leaves the building without a human. Agents draft the follow-up; an attorney approves it; dispatch is a separate press. That boundary is structural, not a prompt instruction.

§03 · in the product today

What pilot firms are working with now.

Not a roadmap. These run on live immigration cases during the pilot.

Client portal

Live

Intake without the email ping-pong.

  • Magic-link access, scoped per party — the petitioner never sees the beneficiary's half
  • Typed answers and uploads held privately until the client presses Submit
  • Fix rounds: request changes, unlock a section, the client resubmits

Document extraction

Live

Reads the documents. Refuses to guess.

  • Passports, green cards, EADs, I-94s, birth and marriage certificates, and more
  • Absent, blank, or illegible → recorded as null, never completed with a plausible value
  • Wrong document in the slot is refused, not misread

The case check

Live

One press answers: is this case complete?

  • Every answer judged complete, incomplete, or undeterminable — never guessed
  • Deterministic crosscheck of the file against the extracted documents
  • Follow-up questions drafted, with a step-by-step trace of what the check did

USCIS knowledge as data

Live

30 visa classifications, verified — not memorized.

  • Required forms, editions, and supporting documents per classification
  • Filing channels verified against official sources, with citations and dates
  • Unverified is labeled unverified. Nothing is inferred

Slack assistant

Live

@yunaki, in the channel your firm already lives in.

  • Case status, timelines, documents, and history — answered with citations to the file
  • Runs the case check on request and reports what it found
  • Drafts client emails; approval cards land in the thread

Human-approved sending

Live

Agents draft. Attorneys send. Always.

  • Every outbound message is a pending draft until a person approves it
  • Recipients only from addresses your firm entered — a client's email can't redirect one
  • Every agent run is recorded: what it read, what it did, in order

§04 · practice areas

One engine. Every paperwork-heavy practice.

Same failure pattern everywhere: high-volume forms, deterministic rules, brutal penalties for small errors.

Immigration

Pilots open

I-130 denials jumped 28.4% → 41.6% in FY 2025.

  • Portal intake, extraction, and the case check on live cases today
  • 30 classifications covered: family, employment, humanitarian, citizenship
  • Form preparation from the checked case record — attorney reviews every field

SSDI / SSI Disability

In development

64% of ~2M annual claims are denied initially. 65% preventable.

  • SGA income math, onset-date vs. DLI validation
  • Form completeness: SSA-3368 · 3369 · 827 · 3373
  • Approval probability score + deficiency list

Bankruptcy

Roadmap

48% of Chapter 13 cases get dismissed. Often on pure arithmetic.

  • Means test (Form 122A/B/C) as pure math
  • Schedule I = Schedule J = means test consistency
  • Credit report vs. listed debts completeness

Workers Compensation

Roadmap

40% of initial claim submissions contain errors. No dedicated tool exists.

  • Injury description = medical bills = CPT codes
  • Wage records vs. employer report reconciliation
  • State fee schedules + timely-filing deadlines

Personal Injury / MVA

Roadmap

The facts inside every demand package, handled deterministically.

  • Intake extraction from crash reports + medical records
  • Chronologies with verified dates and bills
  • Deadline tracking + demand-package assembly

Healthcare Claims

Roadmap

$262B in claims denied annually. 85% preventable.

  • Claim pre-flight for CMS-1500 / UB-04
  • Diagnosis = operative report = medical record
  • Payer-rule and timely-filing validation
one engine: deterministic validation + firm memory. a new practice area is a playbook away, not a new product.
